# Quote Follow-up Pack — Example Home Cleaning (fictional sample)

Editable template-based drafts. Fill every bracketed placeholder and verify the facts before sending. No messages are sent automatically.

## How to use

Use only for an appropriate conversation with someone who requested your services. Stop when they decline or ask you not to contact them. Adapt timing to the conversation, customer preferences and applicable requirements.

Suggested internal checklist: acknowledge the inquiry; send a checked quote; consider one helpful follow-up after two business days; consider a final follow-up after another three to five business days; then close the inquiry. Do not repeat messages when the person has already answered.

## Six email drafts

### 1. New inquiry — acknowledge and gather scope

Subject: Your cleaning inquiry

Hi [First name],

Thanks for contacting Example Home Cleaning (fictional sample). We offer standard home cleaning and recurring maintenance cleaning in Exampletown. To prepare a quote, please share approximate home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, cleaning priorities, and desired frequency. Please leave out access codes and other sensitive information at this stage.

We provide a written quote after reviewing the scope. A booking is confirmed only after we agree on the details.

The Example Home Cleaning team

Based on the details we agreed, your quote is [verified amount] for [precise included scope]. The following items are not included: [actual exclusions, or remove this sentence].

We provide a written quote after reviewing the scope. A booking is confirmed only after we agree on the details.

If the scope looks right, reply to this message with your preferred dates; we will confirm availability before booking.

The Example Home Cleaning team

### 3. First follow-up — confirm receipt

Subject: Any questions about your cleaning quote?

Hi [First name],

I’m following up on the quote we sent for [scope] on [date]. Did it reach you, and is there anything you would like clarified? If your needs have changed, tell us what is different so we can review the quote.

Reply to this message with your preferred dates; we will confirm availability before booking.

The Example Home Cleaning team

### 4. Second follow-up — remove uncertainty

Subject: Would a scope adjustment help?

Hi [First name],

Are you still considering cleaning help for [scope]? If you have a question about what is included, frequency, or the booking process, I’m happy to clarify. We will confirm any revised scope and price in writing before booking.

If now is not the right time, no problem—let us know and we will stop following up.

The Example Home Cleaning team

### 5. Close the inquiry politely

Subject: Closing the loop on your cleaning inquiry

Hi [First name],

I’ll close this inquiry for now so we do not keep following up. If you would like to revisit it, reply here and we can check the current scope, price and availability. There is no need to respond if your plans have changed.

Thank you,
The Example Home Cleaning team

### 6. Confirm an agreed booking

Subject: Cleaning booking confirmation — [confirmed date]

Supplies and surfaces: Tell us about any product preferences or surfaces needing special care so we can confirm what is suitable.

## Quote-information checklist

- Confirm the requested service and whether it is within your actual service area.
- Ask for: approximate home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, cleaning priorities, and desired frequency.
- Clarify condition, priorities, exclusions and any special handling before pricing.
- Confirm frequency and preferred dates without promising availability.
- Provide written scope and verified price.
- Collect access arrangements privately only after booking.
