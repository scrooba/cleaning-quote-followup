# Quote Follow-up Packs for Cleaning Businesses

Clear, editable replies for people who have already asked your cleaning business for a quote.

## Pilot offer: $49

A personalized template pack for one cleaning business:

- Six email drafts and six short-message drafts.
- A quote-information checklist and a customer FAQ.
- A practical follow-up checklist.
- Editable Markdown and a printable HTML copy.
- One consolidated revision.

Personalization uses your actual business facts, policies and booking instructions. This is a template-based writing service. It does not include sending messages, new leads, account access, or guaranteed bookings.

## See what you receive

Read [the sample pack](SAMPLE-PACK.md). It uses a fictional business and is not a customer testimonial. The sample is free to read and adapt for your own business.

## Pilot interest

Interested? Open an issue using **Pilot interest**. Share only public business details and the kind of help you want. This is a non-binding inquiry, not an order or payment request. Do not post customer information, addresses, passwords, bank information, private contact details or payment information in a public issue. No payment is requested in GitHub issues.

## What this is best for

Independent residential cleaning businesses that receive quote requests and want clearer, consistent replies. The owner still checks every factual claim and sends each message to the appropriate person through their existing tools.

## Boundaries

No invented discounts, fake urgency, made-up reviews, guaranteed results or claims that a slot is available when it has not been checked. Short-message drafts are for appropriate follow-ups to quote inquiries, not cold-text campaigns.
